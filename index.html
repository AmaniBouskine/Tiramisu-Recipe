Tiramisu Recipe 🍰

Tiramisu is a delicious Italian dessert made with coffee ☕, mascarpone cheese, and ladyfingers.

Ingredients 🇮🇹

  • 250g mascarpone cheese
  • 3 eggs
  • 100g sugar
  • 200g ladyfingers
  • 1 cup coffee
  • Cocoa powder

Preparation

  1. Prepare the coffee and let it cool.
  2. Mix the eggs and sugar until creamy.
  3. Add the mascarpone cheese and mix well.
  4. Dip the ladyfingers in the coffee.
  5. Place the ladyfingers in a dish.
  6. Add a layer of the mascarpone mixture.
  7. Repeat the layers.
  8. Sprinkle cocoa powder on top.
  9. Refrigerate the tiramisu for a few hours.

Recipe Information

Preparation Time: 30 minutes

Servings: 6 people

Difficulty: Easy
